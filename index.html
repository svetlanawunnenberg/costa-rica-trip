From the Central Valley to the volcano, up into the cloud forest and down to the Pacific.

Before we go

  • Manuel Antonio park tickets for Fri Nov 13, online only through SINAC. Our tour is included, the $18 entry is not.
  • Monteverde Reserve: book a 7 AM entry with a guide.
  • Download offline Google Maps for Costa Rica and install Waze.
  • Save the itinerary image to your phone in case signal drops.

Eating gluten free

  • Naturally safe staples: corn tortillas, rice and beans, gallo pinto, grilled fish, plantains, fresh fruit.
  • Ask about sauces, breading and shared fryers every time.
  • Restaurant listings below come from celiac review sites. Recent reviews beat old ones; confirm with the server.

On the road

  • Sunset is about 5:15 PM. Arrive before dark every drive day.
  • Gas is full service: "lleno con regular, por favor."
  • Nothing visible in the car, ever.
  • Bills include 10% service. Emergency: 911.
